Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026 · This app is in beta.

Awoo helps you look after your cats and dogs. This page explains what we collect, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • Your pets' details: name, breed, dates, health records, reminders, treats, walks, notes, vet and insurance details you choose to add.
  • Photos and posts you upload.

We don't collect your location. The Nearby and emergency buttons open Google Maps, which may use your location under Google's own privacy policy.

Who can see it

  • Your pets' records are visible only to you and anyone you share your invite code with (for example your partner).
  • Pack posts, your Pack handle, your pet's name, breed and profile photo are visible to everyone signed in to Awoo.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase. It may be stored on servers outside Australia.

Deleting your data

You can delete posts, photos and records in the app. To delete your whole account, email us at the address below and we'll remove it within 30 days.
